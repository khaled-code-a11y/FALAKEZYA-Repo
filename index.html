★ Story & Objectives ★

About FALAKEZYA Team

Knight of Change: Pioneering youth-driven scientific development in Jordan

Who We Are

FALAKEZYA is a group of elite top physics graduates and passionate youth from universities across the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.

Responding to global trends in education and aligning with the national vision led by His Majesty King Abdullah II bin Al Hussein, we launched in October 2018 in Amman to be 'Knights of Change'.

Our Vision

To create an organized youth hub capable of achieving sustainable development in Jordan through positive ideas and correct scientific thinking.

Our Mission

To serve all segments of society by simplifying physics, breaking fear around science, and spreading astronomical awareness.

Our Strategic Objectives

10 clear goals driving our mission to elevate physics education and scientific research in Jordan

1
1. Spreading physics and its applications in Jordan in a fun way to break the stigma of physics being difficult.
2
2. Leveraging top physics graduates to simplify scientific concepts for minds of all ages and backgrounds.
3
3. Acting as a permanent bridge between physics enthusiasts and top academic experts.
4
4. Securing support to execute larger, more advanced scientific experiments.
5
5. Expanding outreach to schools and universities to instill a love for physics in students.
6
6. Reaching refugees and underprivileged communities to ensure equal access to modern physics knowledge.
7
7. Opening pathways for future scientific research in physics and securing support for research projects.
8
8. Supporting youth scientific projects and connecting innovators with concerned national authorities.
9
9. Partnering with universities and schools for joint physics events and academic enrichment.
10
10. Continuously expanding our dreams and efforts to elevate physics across Jordan and the Arab world.
